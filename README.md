# Three settings checklist

Claude asks you a few questions about your business and writes your Instructions for Claude, ready to paste in. Then it walks you through checking memory, importing from ChatGPT and reading what it remembered.

## Use it

Open `SETUP-PROMPT.md`, copy the text in the box into a new chat in Claude, Claude Code or Codex, and follow along.

## The three settings

- **1. Instructions for Claude.** In Settings, on the Account tab. Who you are, what you sell, how you like answers. It applies to all your chats.
- **2. Memory.** Settings, then Memory. Check Generate memory from chats is on, then read what it remembered and fix it in the Tell Claude what to change or remove box.
- **3. Import.** Moving from ChatGPT? Settings, then Memory, then Start import. Imports are experimental, so read what came across.
- **Want a chat it won't remember?** Use incognito: the ghost icon on a new chat. It is not saved to your history or memory.
