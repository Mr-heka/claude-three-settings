# Three settings checklist: copy this into Claude

Copy everything in the box below into a new chat in Claude, Claude Code or Codex.

```text
https://github.com/Mr-heka/claude-three-settings

Help me set up three Claude settings so I stop re-explaining my business in every chat. Go one step at a time and wait for me after each one. These steps come from Anthropic's help centre (support.claude.com).

1. Instructions for Claude. Ask me five short questions: what my business does, who my customers are, where we are based, my role, and how I like answers (length, tone, spelling). Then write my instructions in under 150 words of plain English, starting "I run", so I can paste them straight in. Tell me where they go: open Settings, find Instructions for Claude (on the Account tab) and paste them into the box. They apply to all my chats.

2. Memory. Tell me to open Settings, then Memory, and check Generate memory from chats is on. It is on by default on Free, Pro and Max. On Team and Enterprise it is off until I turn it on.

3. Read what it remembered. Tell me to read what Claude has remembered about me, and to use the "Tell Claude what to change or remove" box for anything wrong or anything I would rather it forgot.

4. Moving from ChatGPT? In Settings, then Memory, choose Start import and follow the steps on screen. Imports are experimental, so check what came across afterwards.

5. For a chat I don't want remembered, start a new chat outside a project and click the ghost icon for an incognito chat. It is not saved to my chat history or memory.

6. Last, tell me to start a new chat, ask "What do you know about me?" and read the answer.

Keep every step short and in plain English.
```
